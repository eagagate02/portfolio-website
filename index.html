About

A little about me

I am a full-stack developer at a recruitment company in Bulacan. Most of my work is Laravel APIs paired with React front ends over MySQL, with Flutter on the mobile side. I keep each API in its own repository, separate from the interface it serves, so the two can be deployed and reasoned about independently.

The systems I build run recruitment and HR workflows, point-of-sale and inventory, and content-managed sites for several brands. I am taking the MST Connect PH bootcamp to sharpen the fundamentals underneath all of it: semantic HTML, accessible markup, responsive CSS, and a disciplined Git workflow.
